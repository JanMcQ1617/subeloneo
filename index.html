03 — The Duo

Phantom
& Freddy

José Carlos "Phantom" Cruz and Freddy Montalvo Jr. met in a Massachusetts recording studio and bet everything on a move back to Puerto Rico to make music full-time. The bet paid off: Noah Assad, CEO of Rimas Entertainment, signed them — and the hits haven't stopped since.

Blending reggaeton, trap, pop and R&B into a signature sound, their placements have generated over 30 billion streams worldwide and a Grammy for YHLQMDLG.

Phantom

Melody · Rhythm

Drummer-turned-producer. The drum patterns and melodic instincts that define the duo's sound.

Freddy

Mix · Master

The engineer's ear. Mixing and mastering precision that makes every record radio-ready.

"What I like most about collaborating with Súbelo NEO is how talented they are."
— Bad Bunny, via Apple Music
